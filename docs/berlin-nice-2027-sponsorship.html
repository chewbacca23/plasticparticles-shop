The ride

Berlin to Nice (Nizza), spring 2027. South through Europe to the Riviera. Climbing days, café kilometres, a finish on the coast. Three or four riders, one clean line on the map.

The ask

One ride, a clean stack of partners. Each brand owns its lane. Credit stays clear on kit, site, and film.

  • Colnago or Pinarello: 3-4 bikes in the best constellation for this route (loan preferred, or supported dealer purchase)
  • Shimano: groupsets / drivetrain / wheels in that same constellation (Dura-Ace or Ultegra Di2 class for long spring days)
  • Wahoo: computers and sensors for the crew (ELEMNT / BOLT class), plus route and data support where it fits
  • Oakley: eyewear for the ride south (performance glasses for mountain and Riviera light)

We are ready to sit with German partners and brand teams and make this simple. Henrik already works with people in this world (Wahoo, Oakley, Shimano on the friends list of The Soul Searchers).

What you get

  • A designed ride story across The Soul Searchers (site, postcards, calendar) and our channels
  • Photography and short film worthy of the bikes and kit (design-led)
  • Clear partner credit on kit, site, and film. Each brand in its own lane
  • Access to Lodown-era and brand-media networks for placement where it fits
  • A finish in Nice that photographs like a dream for road culture

Why us

Not a club weekend. A small crew with real tour miles and professional taste. The bikes will be ridden hard and shown well.
