  A Little Ride Around Town


  This morning I picked up the magic Doctor Fish, and my red flash finally got a proper spin around town again. We met at the Wasserklops next to the Gedächtniskirche, as Berliners would say, and it was wonderful to see old faces and new ones, people I rode with as a kid and others I knew from growing up.


  There was Brillski, the one and only, who organizes everything all the time. Then there was Olli, chief master of California Sports, where the 26 inch bikes were lined up, including a Skyway 26 inch cruiser that I would once have given my left nut for.


  It took us only about 950 meters before Brillski had his first flat, and we all rolled over to Olli's shop to fix it. Fish and I had other plans and carried on for two more coffees and a small cake. 🍰


  It wasn't much of a ride, but it was wonderful, because as long as you ride, it always is. Cheers, mates! 🚲
