Consider the rest of your life a present, wrapped neatly, sitting slightly out of reach. A perfect bow. A small card. In cursive it says: **do not touch**.

You are tempted. Of course you are. Maybe it is something you need. Maybe something you did not expect. Like most presents, what is inside remains a surprise.

## Peek, don’t panic

Curiosity is allowed. Tear the wrapping only when you’re ready — small peeks count.

A sentence in a journal. A walk without headphones. A conversation that ends without a verdict.

Waiting is not the same as hiding. Sometimes a small peek every now and again is all we need.
