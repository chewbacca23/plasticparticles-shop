The journal is part of the ride. A sentence today can steady a year of tours.

Write three lines after you unclip. Tomorrow, write three more. The page will not choose the road for you, but it will keep you from disappearing into the fog between trips.

## Practice

1. Name one ride or place you have been postponing.
2. Take the smallest honest step toward it today — a date, a train ticket, a tyre check.
3. Leave the rest of the wrap intact until tomorrow.

Come back to the page. The search continues — hopeful, awake, and still rolling across this wonderful planet.
