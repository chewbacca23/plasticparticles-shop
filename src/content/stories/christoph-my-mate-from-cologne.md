Embrace the short ride…

Ever caught yourself thinking your last ride was not long enough?

That it’s not worth to gear up for just one hour?

Does it feel like everyone in your instagramstravabubble does centurys every! Single! Day!?




Don’t let the internet fool you!

Every minute in the bike is a minute we’ll spend!

A minute you got for yourself. 

In the calm of the outside. 

Away from your everyday stress. The deadlines and all the fears and worries that may haunt you. 




A 30k soloride in the early morning that you sneaked out on in the dark while the rest was still sleeping. 

Or the 25k sundowner after a packed day are well worth all the effort it takes to make them become a reality. 

Your body will feel recharged.

Your mind will be cleared. 

You will feel in balance again. 







As a famous sportswear giant once said:




“Just do it!”.
