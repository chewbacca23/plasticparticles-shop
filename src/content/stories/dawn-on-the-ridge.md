You don’t need the whole map. You need the ground under one foot.

This is the hour before anyone asks you who you are supposed to be. The ridge is quiet. The light is only half there. You can still choose the direction without a committee.

## What this picture holds

A beginning that does not shout. Gold on the horizon is enough. Mist in the valley is allowed.

Take the smallest honest step toward the thing you have been postponing. Leave the rest of the wrap intact until tomorrow.

The search is not a race to empty the box. It is learning to live beside it — hopeful, awake, and still moving.
