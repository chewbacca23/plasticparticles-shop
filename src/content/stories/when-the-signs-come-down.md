There are seasons when the route is lit like a race — every turn labeled, every risk explained. Then there are seasons when someone has taken the signs down overnight.

No right. No wrong. Only weather, and the quiet of an empty road.

## Don’t wait for a replacement map

The urge is to freeze until a clearer board appears. That freeze can last years. A better move: pick the next honest kilometre that still feels like you, then roll it while the light is only half there.

GPS can lie. Paper can tear. Your legs still know how to go.

## Keep one riding mate

Tell one trusted person what you’re trying. Not for permission — for company. The search is quieter when someone knows you are still out there, looking at this planet from the saddle.
