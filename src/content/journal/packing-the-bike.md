Packing is the unglamorous half of touring. Get it wrong and you carry a house. Get it right and the bike still feels like a bike.

## Always

- Two tubes, patches, levers, a pump you have used
- Multi-tool, and a spare chain link if you can fit one
- Lights (front and rear) even for “day rides”
- Rain jacket, one warm layer
- Cash / card, ID, phone in a dry place
- Enough food to reach the first bakery

## Usually

- Small lock
- Sunscreen, a cap, a buff
- Mini bottle of chain lube
- A power bank if you navigate on the phone

## Almost never

A second pair of “just in case” shoes. A giant lock. Three jerseys for a two-day trip. The kitchen sink.

Handlebar bag for the things you grab while rolling. Saddlebag or a small rear pack for kit. Frame bag if you have the space. Soft bags beat a heavy rack if you want the bike to still climb.

Weigh it once. Then take one thing out. Tomorrow’s climb will thank you.
