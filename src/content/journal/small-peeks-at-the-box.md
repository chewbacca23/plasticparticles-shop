Curiosity is not the enemy of patience. You can want to know what is around the next bend and still refuse to tear the paper in a panic.

A small peek is enough for many days: a sentence in the café notebook, a spin without headphones, a conversation that ends without a verdict.

## Practice

1. Name one ride or place you have been postponing.
2. Take the smallest honest step toward it today — a date in the calendar, a tyre check, a look at the train.
3. Leave the rest of the wrap intact until tomorrow.

The search is not a race to empty the box. It is learning to live beside it — hopeful, awake, and still rolling across this wonderful planet.
