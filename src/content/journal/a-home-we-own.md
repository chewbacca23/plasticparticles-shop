For a long time this writing lived on someone else’s platform. The words were ours. The menu was not. Stats vanished. The left sidebar rearranged itself without asking.

Riders know that feeling: a camp that is not quite yours. So we built a home.

## What this place is

- A **landing page** that says who we are: cyclists, travellers, searchers
- A **journal** of notes from the saddle — easy to edit, easy to keep
- **Rides** with pictures: climbs, coasts, rest days, the quiet hours between
- Room to grow: more routes, more towns, more of this wonderful planet

## What you can do next

Point `thenewsoulsearchers.de` at the host that serves this site. Come back after a ride and write. This is not a theme rented by the month. It is our base camp.
