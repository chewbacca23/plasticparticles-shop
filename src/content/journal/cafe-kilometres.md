A cycling blog that only counts moving time is lying to you.

Café kilometres are real: you unclip, you lean the bike, you eat, you look at the square. That is travel. That is how you meet a person with a pump. That is how you learn a town.

On a tour, the café is also logistics. Refill bottles. Check the map. Let the rain cell pass. Write three lines in the notebook.

Skip the guilt. The climb after cake is still a climb. The planet is not a Zwift course.

Order the second coffee if the weather is ugly. Tomorrow’s tarmac will still be there.
